# Risk log — reBot B601-RS

Live risks and their controls. A risk moves to `mitigated`/`closed` only with
evidence, never on intent.

| Date | Risk | Detail | Control | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-07-31 | **Unpowered-testing discipline erodes** | The safety layer is only trustworthy if it is exercised. The temptation once hardware works is to test against the arm and let mock coverage rot | Every test in `b601/tests/` runs with the arm unpowered and CI has no hardware; `MockDriver` gains features rather than tests reaching for the real arm | monitoring |
| 2026-07-31 | **Demonstration dataset is irreplaceable** | 120–200 teleop episodes cannot be regenerated — an operator session is gone. Unlike the sim track, `artifacts/` here is NOT disposable | Off-machine backup from the FIRST pilot episode (Day 5, not Day 10); dataset hash recorded in `dataset_card.md`; `datasets/` gitignored but hashes committed | **open — backup target not yet chosen** |
| 2026-07-31 | **Silent physical drift** | A nudged camera mount or drifted calibration produces a systematic position bias that looks like policy error. The sim track's Object-S regression was exactly this shape (+0.078 m, from a stale reference) | `pin_check.sh` gates camera serials AND calibration hash on every launch; Day-3 calibration report records measured error at known table points | monitoring |
| 2026-07-31 | **`safe_home` trajectory is unguarded** | Homing is a joint-space trajectory; `SafeDriver` sees its endpoints, never its waypoints. As of 2026-08-01 the endpoints ARE guarded: positive finite timeout, pose-reading and joint-state precondition, `max_joint_velocity` passed down, fault latching, arrival at the approved `HomeContract` verified, motion counted only on success. **Still uncovered: the path between the endpoints, timeout enforcement (passed to the driver, not policed), acceleration (no cap exists in `MotionLimits`), and collision.** Listed in the `driver/base.py` docstring | Day-2 work against the real driver: confirm the vendor homing routine honours the velocity bound and route, add an acceleration cap once measured, set `home.tolerance_m` from `repeatability.csv`. **No powered homing before that confirmation** | **open — powered-motion blocker** |
| 2026-08-01 | **`eef_pose()` is a computation, not a sensor** | Once FK supplies the pose, the workspace guard, the step guard and the home postcondition are all checking a model, not the arm. A wrong URDF, wrong end-effector frame or wrong joint ORDER yields poses that look plausible and are wrong everywhere except home | `kinematics_check.py` cross-checks placo against Pinocchio (result: 0.000000000 m over 200 vectors — validates frame/order/units, NOT the model). The motor-order correspondence is asserted nowhere and must be confirmed joint-by-joint on Day 2. **Day-3 calibration is the measurement that validates FK against the arm** | **open — no Cartesian motion under power before Day-3 calibration** |
| 2026-08-01 | **The vendor clips where we reject** | `send_action()` clamps to the plugin's own `joint_limits`/`max_relative_target` and logs at debug. Two envelopes now exist, and the vendor's can silently alter a command our chokepoint approved — contrary to Rule 1 ("rejected, not a clamped version of a wild command") | Configure the plugin's limits WIDER than `limits.yaml` so ours bind first, making any vendor clip a bug rather than a backstop. Undecided until the limits are measured | **open** |
| 2026-08-01 | **A green harness is not a passed gate** | The Day-2 gate says "no fault, collision, or manual correction". Software can see faults; it cannot see a collision or a human catching the arm. A repeatability run that reports 20/20 clean is evidence for one third of the gate and silent on the rest | `gate_status()` requires an explicit operator attestation and fails without it, naming what it cannot observe; the run also stops at the first fault so consecutive motions are counted, never totalled across one | monitoring |
| 2026-07-31 | **A guard that cannot see the failure it guards** | Sim precedent: `pin_check` reported "clean" while the released assets were missing, because they were gitignored. A passing check that is structurally blind is worse than no check | Each guard states in its header what it does NOT cover; `pin_check` asserts physical state explicitly rather than inferring it from git | monitoring |
